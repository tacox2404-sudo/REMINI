Executive summary · Why now
One-off edits are easier to find than ever, and Remini's next step is value that carries on after the first result
The market
One-off AI edits are becoming a commodity
Viral moments are powerful but short
New users are harder to win
Keeping revenue over time is the opportunity: net revenue retention averaged 87%
Remini today
5% of installs start a trial: large room to grow
About 98% of daily users installed earlier: a large, engaged base
Subscribers stay about 4 weeks
The users
Two kinds of users, one need: Fixers (at least 70% of installs) bring big jobs, Creators (up to 20%) small ones
Both need something that carries on after the first result
