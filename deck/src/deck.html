Market
Four forces make one-off edits and viral waves a weaker base for growth
ForceWhat I foundWhat it means for Remini
One-off AI edits are becoming a commodityChatGPT's image tool added about 1M users in one hour (Mar 2025); Nano Banana brought 10M+ new Gemini users and 200M+ edits in about a week (Aug 2025)A single effect or edit is easy to copy and increasingly free elsewhere
Viral moments are powerful but shortBending Spoons created six viral spikes for Remini after 2023, each large and short-livedRemini knows how to win attention, but each wave fades
New users are harder to winQ2 2026: new users are harder to acquire while existing users behave as beforeGrowth that relies on more installs gets more expensive
Keeping revenue over time is the opportunityRemini's net revenue retention averaged 87% from Q1 2023 to Q1 2026Revenue from each group of users shrinks over time
The value has to come from giving people more reasons to use Remini and to keep paying for it

The problem, and why now
Remini delivers great results for a single task, but offers little that carries on after it
Where value can grow
Most people use Remini and leave before a trial
Trial users often finish their task before deciding whether it is worth paying
Subscribers stop once the task is done
Why it matters now
The single task is becoming a commodity
Viral waves fade
New users cost more to win, and revenue from each group of users shrinks over time
Raising the value of each user, today about 22 cents per install, is the growth path that does not depend on more installs or the next viral wave

Impact · Modelling choices
The model is deliberately simple, and what it leaves out is upside
ChoiceHow it is modelled
Timing2 months to build, a test month on 10% of new installs, a rollout month at 50%, then everyone
Discounting9% a year, an estimate from Bending Spoons' debt cost and the equity cost of internet software
CostsOnly what Studio adds: AI compute and storage
Paid weeks+6% = about 1.7 paid days, under half the gap between median and top-quarter first renewal in photo apps (45% vs 53%, RevenueCat 2026)
Returning usersAssumption: 1 in 20,000 a day starts a trial, about 730 a day at launch, fading 5% a month; the least evidenced input
Invites and adsInvites add about 0.3% more installs; ads move by a few dollars a day
Left out, all upsideInvites by invited friends, people who come back after cancelling, cheaper internal compute

Operational feasibility
Operationally manageable: faces, sharing and compute each have a clear rule
Operational riskHow I would handle it
Other people's facesOnly the person can add their own face; they are notified when it is used and can remove it. Friends by invite only, like a shared album, not a public feed
Privacy of saved facesThe same consent and deletion rules as AI Photos, with a clear "delete my profile" control
Inappropriate contentProjects are private to their members; shared styles are recipes within Remini
Compute and storage costGrows with use; free users keep their limits and trials are time-limited. The main swing is the video share: at 30% of creations instead of 10%, the Balanced scenario falls to $4.1M
Building itExisting product teams, reusing existing models and profiles
