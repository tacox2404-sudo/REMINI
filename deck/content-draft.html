What I would change, and questions for you

  • McKinsey guide. The PDF is a set of strategy articles more than a slide manual. Two things I took from it: its exhibit format (full-sentence title, then a small subtitle with the metric and unit, then source notes), now used on every chart slide of the design draft; and the ten questions of "Have you tested your strategy lately?". An optional appendix slide could answer them in one line each (for example, "Does it put you ahead of trends? Yes: single edits are commoditising and creating together is becoming normal"). I would keep it only if you expect a strategy-heavy panel
  • Prototype example vs Truth example. The Truth doc says "5 of 17 done, finish your trip". The prototype now hits the limit in the Family archive ("5 of 12 restored"). I used the prototype wording on the Show slides and in the journey table, so words and screens match. The idea is the same; tell me if you would rather keep the Truth wording
  • One subtle number choice. The Excel reports revenue per install two ways: without invites (Summary row 17: +14% / +16.6% / +21.2%) and with invites (Hurdle tab: Balanced +16.9% against the +16.0% bar). I compare against the bar with invites included everywhere, since the bar includes them. The bar itself shifts slightly by scenario (15.1% / 16.0% / 17.9%) because margin differs; I quote 16% as the Balanced bar
  • Small Excel fix. Summary insight 4 ends mid-sentence ("Invites (about 0.3% more installs)"). Worth completing before anyone opens the file
  • Candidates to cut if you want it tighter: merge slides 13 and 14 (profit formulas and returning users), merge 31 and 32 (scenarios and where the value comes from), and cut Show slide 28 if the live demo follows
  • Title of the deck. I propose "Remini Studio · bigger jobs worth paying for". Alternative: "From one result to work that keeps going"

13Remini today: money
New installs bring $70,000 a day in subscriptions, and ads add only cents per free user
  • Subscriptions: 350,000 × 5% trial × 20% conversion × 4 weeks × $5 = $70,000 a day, about $25.6M a year
  • Ads: 332,500 free users × 3.8 ads × $0.005 = about $6,300 a day, 8% of new-user revenue
  • A free user is worth cents; a trial is worth about $4 (20% × $20)
Source: case brief; ads on actual usage (one ad per action after the first)
14Remini today: returning users
About 98% of daily users installed earlier, and very few of them pay
  • 15M daily users, of which 350,000 are new installs (2.3%)
  • About 98,000 people paying at any moment (3,500 a day × 28 days), 0.65% of daily users
  • A large base already uses Remini every day without paying

17Levers: correlation
The levers move together, so the change has to come from behaviour, not from forcing one number
  • More installs without a better experience mean lower conversion, with no real gain
  • The model shows it: Trial rush lifts trials by 20% but converts worse, and earns less per install than Balanced
  • So Studio acts on the whole experience: trial start, conversion, paid weeks and invites together

33Impact: margins
Studio's real cost is margin: AI creation costs about 25 times an enhancement
  • Per action: enhancement $0.0024 · AI creation $0.060 (90% images at $0.039, 10% short videos at $0.25)
  • AI creation is 74% of AI cost in Balanced
  • Margin after AI compute and storage: 89% today, 85.7% in Balanced, worth −$1.5M of NPV
  • Bounded by the free limits and the 7-day trial; costs at public list prices, so the real margin may be higher
Source: Remini_Studio_Impact.xlsx, Margins and Inputs
